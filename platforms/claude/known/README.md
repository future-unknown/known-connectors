# Known

Shared memory for teams. Give every AI agent a foundation of organizational understanding. Known preserves your company's knowledge, decisions, and history so agents can work with context from the start.

Your team and your agents read and write the same wikis of pages and tables. Ask what your company knows, and the answer comes from Known with links to the pages and records it used. Ask to write something down, and it lands in the right wiki, versioned, with who wrote it.

What you can do:
- Find answers in your company's wikis: plans, decisions, customers, processes.
- Write and edit pages, and keep tables (issues, a CRM, a fundraise) whose fields a schema checks on every write.
- See how any page or record changed over time, and what links to it.
- Publish small sites on Known that sign your team in and read your wikis.

Sign in, connect your agent, and the knowledge starts keeping itself. You sign in to Known in your browser and choose the organizations the assistant may use; it acts as you, within what you can already read and write, and Known records every change it makes as yours, made through the assistant.

Limits: the assistant works only in the organizations you choose when connecting. Members and permissions are managed in Known itself.

## Install

```
/plugin marketplace add future-unknown/known-connectors
/plugin install known@known
```

Then run `/mcp`, choose **known**, and sign in to Known in your browser. There is no key. Ask Claude to get started (`/known:get-started`) to see what's in your organization and take a first step.
