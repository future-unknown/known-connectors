# Get started with Known

Known is shared memory for teams: it gives every AI agent a foundation of organizational understanding, preserving the company's knowledge, decisions and history in wikis of pages and tables that the team and its agents read and write together, with every change versioned and attributed.

1. **See what's there.** List the wikis Known gives you, with each one's description and who reads it. Tell the person in two or three sentences what their organization holds. A new organization has one wiki, `general`, with a short home page.
2. **Offer one first step**, fitted to what you found:
   - **Write down how the team works:** ask three or four questions (what the company does, who is on the team, how decisions are made), then propose a page for `general` and write it once they agree.
   - **Track issues:** set up an issues table and an issues site by following https://github.com/future-unknown/skills/blob/main/skills/issue-tracking/SKILL.md, which asks a few questions and waits for approval before writing.
   - **Find something:** search their wikis and answer with links to the pages and records you used.
3. **Write only what they agreed to.** Read a page before changing it, show what you will write, and report the links Known returns.

If you have no tools from Known in this conversation, say so and ask the person to select Known for their next message. If Known isn't connected to their account at all, https://known.info/setup.md has the steps for each assistant.
